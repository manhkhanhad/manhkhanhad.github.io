I am a PhD student (2025 intake) at the [School of Computing and Information Systems](https://scis.smu.edu.sg/), [Singapore Management University](https://www.smu.edu.sg/), supervised by [Prof. Hady W. Lauw](https://www.hadylauw.com/). My PhD studies are fully funded by the Vingroup Young Talent Scholarship.

My current research focuses on **Large Language Models** and **Generative Recommendation Systems**.

Previously, I obtained my Bachelor's degree in Computer Science (Honors) from the [University of Information Technology](https://www.uit.edu.vn/), Vietnam National University Ho Chi Minh City (2019–2023), under the supervision of [Dr. Vinh-Tiep Nguyen](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=DulHk_YAAAAJ&hl=en).
